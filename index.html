How to play

Read a stage. Short sections, each with a formula and a worked example.
Play the labs. Step through the field work and watch the numbers respond.
Clear the checkpoints. First-try answers build your closed traverse.
Beat each boss. The optional finale is the whole course in one problem.

Based on the CSURV2210 lecture decks and CSURV221P lab manual. The optional World 6 uses standard textbook content for the mass diagram, GPS and Simpson’s rule, because no slides exist for them. Progress is saved in this browser only. Inside a world, the Teacher button shows answers and unlocks its boss for demos.
